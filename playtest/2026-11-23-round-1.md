# Playtest round 1: 2026-11-23

Owner's playtest of v0.1 (the build at commit `0e9ee6a`). Their notes, grouped; the proposals they led to come after.

## Feedback

### Feel and visuals
- Player feedback isn't great. Jacking in should take a moment: the handshake should show progress, like an access sequence, instead of landing you straight on the run screen.
- The background city isn't cyberpunk enough.
- Vesna should contact you through a cyberpunk terminal, maybe as a wireframe character.
- The market should have character.
- The juice on a successful download can be turned up a lot.

### Rules
- The rules are pretty good.
- The first rolls of a first visit are meaningless: with nothing downloaded, a miss costs only time, so there's no decision. Give the player one block in each file from the start.
- If the player jacks out and comes back, turn the last block of each unfinished file back to unbanked, so the player always has a stake going in.
- Instead of jacking out, the player could spend some money and some time to reroute and secure the current download without leaving the server.
- Time is a fine clock and Vesna is good pressure, but trace could be another kind of pressure. Wants options.
- Dice could have more kinds of faces, like dual-color faces or other benefits.
- The reward for an all-red file seems low for how hard it is. "But maybe I just need to get better at the game."

### Clarity
- Upgrading dice isn't clear. It took a while to find the dice lab, and they weren't sure they could upgrade at all.

## What I saw playing the same build

- **Jacking in:** one click cuts straight to the run screen. The handshake is a single sentence ("The handshake took 50 minutes"), so the biggest time cost in the game happens off screen.
- **A full download:** the screen dims and a dialog says "Download complete", with the payout as a row in a table. Behind it the console briefly reads "No signal… 0% chance to hit" for a server that's already finished.
- **Market:** the whole market panel replays its fade-in on every click, so it flickers whenever you select or buy something. It's a list of forms with no character.
- **Dice lab:** it takes three clicks (Market, a die, a face) before any upgrade or price appears. The dice in the deck panel are the obvious thing to click, but clicking one only seats or unseats it.
- **Vesna:** a text card at dawn. She never shows up during the night.
- **City:** the skyline is plain boxes with amber windows. It reads as "a city at night", not cyberpunk.

### The stake rule, simulated

I modelled the proposed stake rule outside the game: every unfinished file starts each run with one block at risk (a free one on a first visit, the last banked block on a return). Average minutes to finish a server with the reference player, three standard dice:

| Server | Now | Stake rule | Free block every run |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red 4 | 192 | 798 | 180 (by never rolling) |
| Red 6 | 284 | 2,137 | 270 (by never rolling) |
| Blue 8 | 262 | 558 | 282 |
| Green 12 | 207 | 222 | 204 |
| Red 3 + blue 6 | 201 | 343 | 186 |
| Blue 8 + green 12 | 159 | 225 | 154 |

- A lone red file hits only 42% of rolls with the starting dice. Risking one block per run at under 50% means the file drifts backwards on average, so a player who leaves when the odds are against them never finishes it.
- Green files barely change: they hit too often for one block at risk to matter.
- A free block every run avoids the drift, but on a lone red file the best play becomes "jack in, jack out, keep the free block", which is worse than the meaningless rolls it replaces.

## Decisions

Proposed after this round and chosen by the owner:

- **City background:** none of the three mockups (megacity, wireframe grid, street level). The owner will explore raster art first; the background is on hold.
- **Vesna:** a live point-cloud face in the style of the owner's reference image, built from a portrait the owner supplied. The photo stays out of the repository; only the point cloud made from it ships.
- **Market:** run by a back-room dice maker with her own character, a workbench for your dice and prices shown on every face.
- **Stakes:** the owner's rule as given. Every unfinished file starts each run with one block at risk: a free block if nothing of it is banked, otherwise its last banked block. Lone red files only appear from night 3, priced by simulation.
- **Reroute:** pay money and time to bank the current download without leaving the server.
- **Trace:** a per-server alert. Visits and flatlines raise it; when it fills, the sysadmin purges what you've banked there.
- **New faces:** dual-color, Firewall, Payday and Anchor.
- **Red rewards:** handled by the stake rule and honest pricing rather than a separate bonus.
- **Handshake, download juice and the dice-upgrade path:** Claude's call.

# Playtest round 2: 2026-11-23

Owner's reaction to the round 1 build (commit `bffddff`).

## Feedback

- "The wireframe face for Vesna is atrocious."
- Broken strategy: reflashing a die to all green means you can't lose on green files.
- Wants custom dice for sale instead of upgrades, such as 3 red and 3 blank, or 2 red, 2 blue and 2 blank, with the other faces in the mix.

## Notes

- Vesna's face: it was flat and clumpy (brightness from skin texture, no form, weak features, a lumpy outline). A rebuilt converter in `tools/pointcloud.py` (traced contours, a sculpted lit depth model, an even mesh and a surface haze) reads far better in offline previews, but the owner put graphics on hold before it reached the game. The game still ships the old face.
- Dice: done. Reflashing is gone; Moth sells a catalog of fixed dice, none of which hits one color more than half the time.

## Follow-up: art direction

- "The face of Vesna and the butterfly are both terrible." Both characters should look like a polished game, not something thrown together in ASCII. Not necessarily a wireframe.
- Then: abandon the owner's portrait entirely. Vesna should not be based on the owner's face.
- Notes: Moth's emblem reads as a butterfly (rounded fore and hind wings, no moth cues). Three directions with no real face were mocked up in the real call panel: calling-card emblems (Vesna a swallow, Moth a death's-head hawkmoth), noir silhouettes with neon rim light (Vesna in profile with a cigarette and a cybernetic eye, Moth hooded with glowing moth-eye goggles), and voice-only voiceprints (Vesna a radial spectrum in encryption rings, Moth a mirrored spectrum shaped like wings). Nothing has shipped yet.
- Decision: the owner picked the noir silhouettes, without Vesna's cigarette.

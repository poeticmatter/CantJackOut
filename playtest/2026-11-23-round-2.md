# Playtest round 2: 2026-11-23

Owner's reaction to the round 1 build (commit `bffddff`).

## Feedback

- "The wireframe face for Vesna is atrocious."
- Broken strategy: reflashing a die to all green means you can't lose on green files.
- Wants custom dice for sale instead of upgrades, such as 3 red and 3 blank, or 2 red, 2 blue and 2 blank, with the other faces in the mix.

## Notes

- Vesna's face: it was flat and clumpy (brightness from skin texture, no form, weak features, a lumpy outline). A rebuilt converter in `tools/pointcloud.py` (traced contours, a sculpted lit depth model, an even mesh and a surface haze) reads far better in offline previews, but the owner put graphics on hold before it reached the game. The game still ships the old face.
- Dice: done. Reflashing is gone; Moth sells a catalog of fixed dice, none of which hits one color more than half the time.
